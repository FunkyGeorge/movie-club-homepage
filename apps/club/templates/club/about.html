The short version

A weekly movie club with a long memory.

One pick at a time, we build a shared history of what we watched and what we loved.

How it works

Each week, one member chooses a movie for the group. The pick gets a week of its own, and the club keeps a permanent record of the title, release year, runtime, genres, and dates.

What is public

This site is a public archive. It shows the movies and the shape of the club over time, but it never publishes who selected a particular movie.
